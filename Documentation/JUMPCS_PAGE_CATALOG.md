# JumpCS page catalog

Descriptors cover entry/registration, confirmation, profile, phone/SMS,
account-ready, product, cart, delivery, payment method, card form, review,
3DS, and order completion. Important pages combine JumpCS URL, title variants,
and characteristic selectors such as `#frmOnetime`, `#confirmation_code`,
`#auth_tel`, `#qty/#cart_button`, named card fields, and the exact final button
`注文を確定する`.

CAPTCHA, maintenance, session-expired and access states are classified before
continuing. Order success additionally requires `/shop/order/order.aspx`, a
non-empty `order_id`, title verification, and an order-complete DOM marker.
Screenshots are disabled for credentials, SMS, card, review and 3DS pages.
